## Short Bio

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Computer Science at the University of Stavanger, working at the intersection of Large Language Models, Information Retrieval, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). My research focuses on how language models can use external and internal memory to support more reliable reasoning, planning, and long-term interaction. In particular, I am interested in episodic memory, memory-augmented language agents, multi-hop retrieval, and metacognition: how models can assess what they know, identify uncertainty or missing information, and decide when and how to retrieve additional evidence. My broader goal is to develop language agents that can reason more effectively over past experiences and external knowledge while remaining grounded in retrieved information.
